Project 6

How Rocket Thrust Works

This short scientific animation explains how rocket thrust relates to Newton's Third Law of Motion. Hot exhaust gases are pushed downward from the rocket engine while an equal and opposite force pushes the rocket upward.

Watch on YouTube

Production Process

I used generative AI as the starting point for the visual animation and then brought the animation into Adobe Premiere Pro for human-in-the-loop editing and scientific clarification.

In Premiere, I added instructional text at precise timecodes, animated the position of the image using keyframes, incorporated fade effects, and recorded my own voice-over. These manual adjustments allowed me to control the pacing and make the relationship between downward exhaust and upward thrust easier to understand.

The final animation is intentionally short and uses minimal text, simple graphics, movement, color, and narration to communicate one scientific idea rather than overwhelm the viewer with unnecessary information.
