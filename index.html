Welcome to My E-Portfolio

I am a high school educator currently teaching Pre-AICE World History and AICE Psychology while completing graduate work in Educational Technology at the University of Florida. Before entering education, I earned a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Film and developed experience with video production, editing, and visual storytelling.

This e-portfolio brings those two parts of my background together. It showcases projects completed in EME 6208 that explore visual design, web development, video production, animation, screencasting, and instructional design. My goal is not simply to use technology because it is available, but to determine when and how technology can make learning clearer, more engaging, and more meaningful.
